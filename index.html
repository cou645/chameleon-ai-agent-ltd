Chameleon AI

Local-first desktop tools with AI built in — a code editor, a presentation studio, a teaching classroom, UK accounting, fleet comms and more. Your data stays on your machine; your AI keys stay yours.

Actively developed 🇬🇧 UK business 🔒 Local-first, privacy-first 🐧 Linux · Windows · macOS

One platform, several tools

Chameleon AI is a desktop suite for freelancers and small businesses. Each tool does a real job — writing code, giving a talk, running a class, keeping the books, coordinating a fleet — and each has AI built in where it helps, not bolted on for show.

Everything runs on your own machine. Choose your AI provider at any time — DeepSeek, OpenAI, Anthropic, Groq, Mistral, Kimi — or run a model locally for no cost. Your keys never leave your device.

What they have in common

🔒

Runs on your machine

Your files, ledgers and chat history live on your computer. Nothing goes to a third party unless you choose a cloud AI provider.

🔌

Bring your own AI

DeepSeek, OpenAI, Claude, Groq, Mistral, Kimi — one click to switch. Or run a model locally through Ollama or LM Studio for free.

📱

Phone companion

Chat to the AI, control the presenter, reach your files and join video calls from your phone.

🌐

Private connections

Video and voice go directly between devices over your own private network — no service in the middle, no per-minute bill.

🎙️

Voice in and out

Dictate to it and have answers read back — on-device, with no cloud transcription.

🐧

Linux, Windows, macOS

One codebase, all three platforms.
