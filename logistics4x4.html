Logistics 4×4

Cross-border fleet communication for small operators. Get the driver at the border, head office, the customs officer and the customer abroad all into one conversation at once — on a normal Android phone, with live maps, cargo manifests, translated voice chat and emergency alerts.

📞 Four phone callers + four video peers in one call 🗺️ Live maps, works offline 🧭 Offline compass to every driver 🌍 15 languages, translated live 📋 Manifests + proof of delivery 🚨 Fleet-wide emergency alerts

One call, everyone in it

Phone calls and video share a single conversation — up to eight people

📞 Up to four phone callers

  • Ordinary mobile calls — no app, no data needed
  • The customs officer in the booth
  • The customer in another country
  • Head office on a landline

📹 Up to four video callers

  • Connects phone to phone directly — no video service, no bill
  • Everyone sees and hears everyone
  • A second driver in the convoy
  • The dispatcher, and a remote team member
Sound from the phone calls is merged into the group call by the phone itself — earpiece only, private.

Logistics 4×4 isn't trying to replace the big proprietary platforms the large courier networks run. It fills the gap those tools can't reach — a driver at a remote crossing who needs head office, a second driver three miles back, the inspector in the booth, and a customer abroad who can only dial in by phone, all connected in seconds, with translated chat everyone can read.

Who it's for

High-stakes situations with little or no infrastructure

🛃

Border crossing

The driver connects the customs officer (phone call), head office (video), the receiving customer (phone call) and a second driver in convoy (video) — all at once, with translated chat visible to everyone.

🚚

Long-haul convoy

Trucks spread over fifty miles stay in one shared call with push-to-talk, everyone's position on the same live map, and routes sent by the office arriving straight on the driver's screen.

🏕️

Off-road and trekking

No signal? The offline compass points to every other fleet member. Pins mark fuel stops, hazards and meeting points. With directional aerials, trucks can stay linked over fifty miles apart.

🌐

Multilingual teams

Hold the talk button and speak; it works out your language, translates, and delivers text and audio to each listener in their own — fifteen languages, all handled on the phone itself.

📦

Proof of delivery

Scan the depot code to load the manifest, scan the customer's code on delivery. Refused? Take a photo and a written note. Capture the customer's signature on screen.

🏢

Dispatch office

The dispatcher sees every driver on the live map, works the run sheet, sends manifests and routes to individual drivers, and broadcasts alerts to the whole fleet.

The map

Street, satellite and hybrid views — with everything cached for offline use

🗺️

Three views, one tap

Switch between street map, satellite imagery and a hybrid (satellite with road names on top). Anywhere you've looked before loads again with no signal — the tiles are kept on the phone.

📍

Every driver on the map

Anyone who shares their location shows as a live pin. Tap one for their name and arrival time. The dispatcher sees the whole fleet at a glance — no separate tracking service.

📁

Routes sent from the office

The office sends a route straight to a driver's phone and it appears on the map as a coloured line, instantly. Recent routes are kept for re-use. China map-offset is corrected automatically.

🧭

Compass navigation with no signal

Lost data? A full-screen compass shows a bearing arrow and distance to each driver, from their last known position — useful for off-road convoys and mountain rescue. The map can also rotate with the phone for heads-up driving, or lock north-up.

Alerts and the run sheet

🚨

Emergency broadcast

Send a fleet-wide alert in one action — it lands on every screen as a coloured banner, with separate looks for an emergency, an instruction, important news and general info.

📋

Manifest and run sheet

The office pushes each driver their stops and cargo list. The driver works down the run sheet; the office sees progress in real time.

✍️

Exceptions, captured

A refused or partial delivery is recorded on the spot — photo, note and reason — so there's no argument later.
