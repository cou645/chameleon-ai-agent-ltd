Chameleon Media Center

A full desktop media studio built on mpv — playback, playlists, YouTube & Bilibili search with a watch-as-you-go cache, live streaming, DVB-T/T2 TV, DVD/Blu-ray/CD, Bluetooth & USB audio routing, sound effects, a visualizer, a video editor, an equalizer, virtual webcams, and multi-phone Android mirroring, in one app.

🎞️ mpv-powered playback 🔎 YouTube & Bilibili search + watch cache 📡 Live DVB-T/T2/C/S/ATSC TV 💿 DVD · Blu-ray · CD 🔵 Bluetooth Tx/Rx + USB turntable/mixer input 📱 Up to 16 phones mirrored at once 📷 Virtual webcam + 4-corner PiP 📡 Stream to browsers, YouTube, Twitch & more 🤏 Pinch/scroll zoom on each phone 🤖 MCP server included

What's inside

Thirteen tabs, one media engine

🎞️

Video panel

Playback powered by mpv — wide format support, smooth seeking, a one-click fullscreen toggle (or F11), automatic recovery if the player process ever dies unexpectedly, and it remembers whichever audio output device you last picked across restarts instead of resetting to a default. A low-latency picture-in-picture overlay puts up to four cameras — the built-in webcam or any phone — in the corners of whatever's playing. Record a playing stream, or download what's playing, with one key.

📃

Playlist

Drag-and-drop reordering, queue management, playlist import and save, and restore a deleted playlist from the trash. Add a single file or a whole folder, subfolders included. Entries whose file isn't there — a USB drive that isn't plugged in, a cached video you cleaned up — grey out until it's back, and a cleaned-up video re-downloads with a double-click.

🔎

Search & watch cache

Search YouTube or Bilibili, list a TikTok creator's latest videos, or paste a TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Snapchat Spotlight or Douyin link. Queue results in a numbered watch list under the playlist. Whatever you watch is kept on disk as you watch it — no separate download — so the next viewing plays from the local copy, and a partly watched video shows how much is saved and fills in the rest next time. Move saved videos to the main playlist, and pick which ones to keep or delete when you close the app. Optional browser cookies get past "confirm you're not a bot" and sign-in walls.

📡

DVB TV

Scan and watch real digital TV — DVB-T/T2, DVB-C, DVB-S/S2 and ATSC — straight from a USB or PCI tuner.

💿

DVD / CD / Blu-ray

Detects what's in the optical drive automatically and plays DVD-Video, Blu-ray or Audio CD tracks — no separate disc software needed.

🔵

BT/USB audio

Route playback to one or several Bluetooth speakers at once (PulseAudio combine-sink), flip the adapter into receive mode so the PC itself becomes a Bluetooth speaker, or loop in any other PulseAudio capture source — a USB turntable, MiniDisc deck, DJ mixer/controller, or audio interface — straight into playback and the Visualizer, all from one Input Sources checklist.

📱

ADB Phone — up to 16 at once

A grid of 1–16 independent panels in as many columns as you like — mirror and control that many Android phones simultaneously, each with its own screen, audio routing and input. Pinch (this box has a touchscreen) or scroll-wheel to zoom into any phone's screen, Shift+drag to pan. Tap, swipe and type directly with mouse, stylus, touch or your physical keyboard — a persistent per-device shell keeps every input real-time instead of paying a fresh connection cost per keystroke. One-click "Connect Wireless" switches a USB-connected phone to adb-over-wifi automatically, or pair over Wi-Fi by scanning the phone's pairing QR code. Push and pull files to any phone, and a dozing phone is woken before streaming starts.

📷

Virtual cameras

A "CMC PC Webcam" device that any video-call app, browser or OBS can pick — feed it the built-in camera or any connected phone's camera, and switch sources mid-call without the app losing the device. Each phone panel also gets its own virtual camera.

📡

Live Stream

Stream a camera or the video panel to any browser on your LAN or Tailscale (HLS player page) plus a low-latency feed for TrXi-Ctrl, VLC or mpv — many viewers from one encode — or push it to YouTube Live, Facebook Live, Twitch, WeChat Channels or any RTMP server.

🔗

Share & discover

CMCs on the same network find each other automatically, and a group code shares your playlist — local files included — with other CMCs.

🔔

Sound Effects

A soundboard for on-the-fly audio cues while you play or stream.

🌈

Visualizer

Real-time audio-reactive visuals for anything currently playing.

✂️

Video editor

A frame-accurate clip sequencer: set In/Out frames from the player, reorder clips, add a music track (mixed under or replacing the original sound) and subtitles (burned in or as a switchable track), then export in the background.

🎚️

18-band equalizer & audio/video effects

Shape the sound to the room, or the mood, with presets you can tweak and save — plus audio effects and a dedicated video effects tab.

✅

Notes

Typed or handwritten notes (stylus/touch-friendly) right alongside your media — useful for production/session notes while you work.

🔒

Lock, themes & preferences

Lock the window with a pattern (Ctrl+Alt+L), switch themes, and set your preferences — with built-in About and Help.

📲

Remote control

Every play/pause/seek/volume action is exposed over mpv's own IPC socket, which TrXi-Ctrl — our free phone app — talks to directly for real transport control, not just a session list. Closing a session from the phone closes the whole app cleanly rather than leaving a half-dead player behind.

🤖

MCP server

A bundled Model Context Protocol server exposes playback status, play/pause/seek/volume/open and library browsing as tools — so an AI agent and your phone always see and control the same session.

Built on open foundations

PySide6 (Qt) desktop app, mpv playback engine

🖼️

PySide6 / Qt

A native, responsive desktop UI — part of the same Python/Qt stack as the rest of the Chameleon AI desktop apps.

🎬

mpv engine

Embedded via mpv's own window-embedding and JSON IPC socket — the same reliable, widely-used playback engine behind many Linux media tools, with libdvdnav/libdvdread/libcdio/libbluray already linked in for disc playback.

🔊

PulseAudio-native audio routing

Multi-device output uses PulseAudio's own module-combine-sink; any capture source — Bluetooth receive or a USB audio device — loops in via module-loopback, checking first for routing PulseAudio's own Bluetooth policy already set up rather than fighting it.
