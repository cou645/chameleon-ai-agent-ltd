Chameleon Companion

Your Chameleon AI desktop, on your phone — chat to its AI, control the presenter, reach your files, drop a receipt into your books, and join group video calls, all over your own private network.

🤖 Talk to your desktop's AI 📹 Group video, up to four 📞 Merge in a phone call 📂 Reach your desktop files 🧾 Receipt → your ledger 🎭 Presenter remote

What it does

One app, paired to your Chameleon AI desktop

🤖

AI chat, by voice

Speak a question; it goes to your desktop's AI, and the answer streams back and is read aloud.

📹

Group video

One tap calls everyone online. The grid adapts to one, two, three or four people. It runs directly between phones over your private network — no video service, no bill.

📞

Phone-call bridge

Merge an ordinary mobile call into the group video call, so someone with no app and no data can still be in the conversation.

🎭

Presenter remote

Drive a Kameleon Presenter talk from your phone — next, previous, push content onto the stage.

🖥️

Screen mirror

See your desktop screen on the phone and tap to control it.

📂

File browser

Browse, open, edit and upload files on the desktop from anywhere on your network.

🧾

Receipts into the books

Photograph a receipt; it goes to the desktop, the AI drafts a ledger entry, and you confirm it. A role-limited dashboard shows the figures — see Accounting.

🎵

Media

Play music and video from a network drive, streamed through the desktop.

🔒

Security alerts

If someone fails your desktop lock screen, you get an alert on your phone with the photo it captured.

🔐

How it connects

Everything runs over your own private network — your local Wi-Fi at home, encrypted from anywhere else. Nothing passes through our servers.
