Chameleon Book64 Narrator

An ePub creation tool and a secure, indexed personal book shelf — with audio narration that reads by word, phrase, sentence, grammatical category or as one flowing voice.

🔊 Audio narration 🌐 Two languages side by side 📚 Imports and exports ePub 🖨 Print and PDF 🔒 Password-protected pages

What you can do with it

Read, listen, write and learn — in one indexed book

🔊

Hear every page

The narrator reads aloud with natural offline voices and highlights the text as it goes. Choose how it moves: word by word, phrase, sentence, flowing reading — or by grammatical category, picking out the verbs, nouns or adjectives.

🗣️

Ideal for language practice

Slow a sentence down, repeat a single phrase, follow the grammar in colour, and hear the same passage in a second language. Ask the built-in AI tutor about any word or line on the page — by text or by voice.

🌐

Two languages in parallel

Keep a translation beside every page and show it above the original, verse by verse or paragraph by paragraph. The matching line lights up in the other language as you read or listen.

📝

Write your own books

Indexed entries for recipes, notes, lyrics, poetry collections, course material and more. Format with a familiar toolbar, group pages into sections, and give each entry a title, icon and comment.

🗂️

Short or long books

Save a short indexed .Sbook64 or a long .Lbook64. A whole book is a single file you can copy, back up or send — embedded images travel inside it.

🖼️

Embedded images

Drop pictures straight into a page; they are stored inside the book. Pack them into the file for sending, or unpack them into a folder for quick editing.

📚

ePub in, ePub out

Import ePub (and Word, OpenDocument, HTML, Markdown and text) and every chapter becomes an editable page. Export your book — or just one of its translations — as an ePub 3 e-book with a table of contents, or print it and save it as PDF.

🔍

Search the whole shelf

Find words and phrases across a book or your whole library without opening each one — even across languages, so an English search can find the matching passage in a translation.

🔒

A private shelf

Lock individual pages with a password; locked pages are never exported or printed. Your books stay on your own computer.

🎨

Comfortable to read

Choose from eighteen themes, including high-contrast light and dark. Fully usable from the keyboard and with screen readers.
