Web Services

From nothing to a live, professional website — built for you from your own material, pointed at your domain, hosted at no monthly cost, and looked after once it's up.

🏗️ Built for you, not a template 🔗 Domain set up properly 🖥️ Hosted free 📬 Enquiries handled 📈 Findable in search
Service 1

🏗️ We build the site

From your project files, documents and a short conversation, we build a complete multi-page site — not a template, a real site about what you actually do. Your colours and logo, mobile-ready, and privacy-first with no trackers or third-party scripts. One round of feedback, then it's yours: all the files and the domain, no lock-in.

  • Home, About, Features, Contact — plus a blog if you want one
  • Content drawn from your own documents
  • Media placeholders you swap in when ready
  • Translated versions if you need them
Service 2

🔗 We set up your domain

The confusing part — registrar logins, the domain's technical settings, the wait for changes to take effect — handled for you where the registrar allows it, or given to you as an exact copy-paste list with a plain explanation of every entry before you touch anything. We confirm the padlock (secure certificate) is live before we call it done.

Service 3

🖥️ We get it hosted

Live at zero monthly cost on GitHub Pages, Cloudflare Pages, Netlify or Vercel — or served straight from your own machine if you would rather keep it in-house. We do the first publish and leave you one command — or one sentence to the Chameleon AI — for every update after.

Service 4

📬 We keep an eye on it

Once it is live, the AI reads incoming enquiries, drafts replies for your approval, collects what you need from each lead, and sends you a weekly summary — visitors, enquiries, and anything urgent flagged straight away. Every lead tracked from first email to signed.

Service 5

📈 We make it findable

Search tags and structured data on every page, a sitemap kept current, submission to Google and Bing, privacy-respecting analytics with no cookie banner, and AI-written articles to pull in search traffic. No ad spend, no agency retainer.

Start to finish

Typically one session to build, same day to go live

1

Discovery call · 15 min

A plain-English questionnaire — business name, what you do, your registrar, colour preferences, pages needed. No web knowledge required.

2

Build · same session

The AI reads your files and builds the full site. You preview it on a private link — no server needed. One round of feedback and it is ready.

3

Publish · ~30 min guided

We put the site online, give you the exact steps to point your domain at it, and watch it go live — usually the same day.

4

Handover + ongoing

A one-page cheat sheet: how to update the site, how to add content, how to read the weekly report. The AI watches it from day one.
