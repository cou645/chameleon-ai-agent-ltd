  • Registered office: 3rd Floor, 86-90 Paul Street, London, EC2A 4NE, United Kingdom
  • Contact for privacy matters: stemsee@chameleon-ai-agent-ltd.net
  • ICO: CHAMELEON-AI-AGENT LTD processes personal data only for its own licensing, software-support and business-administration purposes. Where a data protection fee is payable to the Information Commissioner's Office, its registration number will be published here.

For the personal data described in this policy, CHAMELEON-AI-AGENT LTD is the data controller. The Software is local-first: your books, ledgers and financial records stay on your own device and are transmitted only to HMRC when you file (see sections 5 and 6). We do not receive or process your accounting data, so we are not a data processor for it. Where the Software is used by a business or an accountant to process data about that organisation's own clients, staff or suppliers, that business or accountant is the controller for that data.

2. Scope of this policy

This policy covers:

  • the website at chameleon-ai-agent-ltd.net and its subdomains;
  • the Chameleon AI desktop software and its companion mobile application ("the Software"), including the AI IDE, Presenter, Accounting (Making Tax Digital), Classroom, PC Technician, Logistics 4×4 and related tools;
  • enquiries, licensing, support and other correspondence with us.

Third-party services you choose to connect the Software to — for example an AI provider, HM Revenue & Customs, a bank or a cryptocurrency exchange — are governed by their privacy policies, not this one. Links or integrations to those services do not mean we control how they use your data.

3. Our privacy principles

The Software is designed to be local-first. Your documents, ledgers, code, presentations, chat history and API keys are stored on your own device and are not transmitted to us. We collect the minimum personal data needed to run the website, provide licences and support the Software, and we do not sell personal data or use it for third-party advertising.

5. Data the Software processes on your device

When you use the Software, the following are processed locally on your own computer or phone and are not sent to us:

  • the files you open or create — code, documents, presentations, media;
  • your accounting records, ledgers, invoices, VAT figures and supporting data;
  • your AI provider API keys, and the prompts and responses exchanged with the provider you choose;
  • credentials or tokens you enter for third-party services (HMRC, banks, payment processors, cryptocurrency exchanges);
  • chat history, settings and local caches.

This data is transmitted only when you direct the Software to contact a third party — for example asking a cloud AI provider a question, syncing a bank feed, or submitting a return to HMRC — and in each case it goes directly from your device to that third party over an encrypted connection. You are responsible for securing your own device, backups and credentials.

5.1 Bank feeds (Open Banking)

If you choose to connect a bank account, the Software uses a regulated Open Banking provider — an FCA-authorised Account Information Service Provider (AISP), for example GoCardless Bank Account Data or TrueLayer — to retrieve your account and transaction data. You authenticate directly with your bank; the AISP then passes the transaction data to the Software on your device, where it is added to your local ledger. That data, and the access tokens for the connection, are held on your device and by the AISP under the AISP's own terms and privacy notice. CHAMELEON-AI-AGENT LTD does not receive, store or have access to your bank data or bank credentials. Open Banking consents expire (typically after 90 days) and you can withdraw a connection at any time through your bank or the AISP.

6. HM Revenue & Customs — Making Tax Digital

The Accounting tool can submit VAT and other tax information to HMRC through HMRC's Making Tax Digital APIs. When you use this feature:

6.1 What is sent to HMRC

  • Your VAT Registration Number (or other tax reference), the return figures you have prepared (the nine VAT boxes or equivalent), the obligation/period key, and your declaration that the figures are final.
  • OAuth tokens issued by HMRC to authorise the connection.
  • Fraud-prevention header data that HMRC requires every Making Tax Digital application to send. This identifies the device and connection making the submission and can include: a device identifier generated by the Software, your device's local and public IP addresses, screen and window size, timezone, operating-system user name, and the product name and version. HMRC uses this data to detect and prevent fraud, as described in HMRC's own fraud prevention guidance.

6.2 How it is sent

All of the above is transmitted directly from your device to HMRC over a secure connection. It is not routed through, stored by, or accessible to CHAMELEON-AI-AGENT LTD. HMRC access tokens are stored only on your device.

6.3 Lawful basis and responsibility

Processing for HMRC submissions is carried out to comply with a legal obligation (your tax filing duties) and on the basis of the contract under which you use the Software. HMRC is an independent controller of the data once received; its handling is governed by the HMRC Privacy Notice. You remain responsible for the accuracy and timeliness of everything you submit; the Software is a filing tool and does not provide tax or accountancy advice.

7. Cookies and similar technologies

The website does not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. It may use a small number of strictly necessary items (for example remembering your language choice) that are exempt from consent under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations. The Software uses local storage on your device for settings and caching, as described in section 5. If we introduce any non-essential cookies in future we will ask for your consent first and update this policy.

8. Who we share personal data with

We share personal data only as needed to run our business:

  • Hosting and email providers that operate the website and our correspondence infrastructure on our behalf, under contract and appropriate safeguards.
  • Payment and accounting providers used to take payment for commercial licences and to keep our statutory books.
  • Professional advisers (accountants, lawyers) where necessary and under a duty of confidentiality.
  • Authorities, regulators or law enforcement where we are required to do so by law, or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.
  • A successor entity if the business is reorganised, sold or transferred, subject to this policy.

Third-party services you connect the Software to yourself (AI providers, HMRC, banks, exchanges) receive data directly from your device under their own terms; we are not a party to that transfer.

9. International transfers

Some providers we use, and some AI providers you may choose, are located outside the United Kingdom. Where personal data for which we are responsible is transferred outside the UK, we rely on UK adequacy regulations or on standard contractual clauses / the UK International Data Transfer Agreement, together with appropriate technical safeguards.

10. How long we keep personal data

  • Enquiries that do not lead to a contract: kept only as long as needed to deal with the enquiry, then deleted. We do not keep a prospect or marketing list.
  • Licensing, contract and support records: for the life of the relationship and then up to 7 years, to meet accounting, tax and limitation-period requirements.
  • Website server logs: retained by our hosting provider for a short period for security and diagnostics.

Data processed only on your device is kept until you delete it.

11. How we protect personal data

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures, including encryption in transit, access controls, least-privilege administration, and keeping the number of people who can access personal data to a minimum. No system is perfectly secure; you are responsible for the security of your own device, credentials and backups.

Under UK data protection law you have the right to: be informed; access a copy of your personal data; have inaccurate data corrected; have data erased in certain circumstances; restrict or object to processing; data portability; and to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. To exercise any of these, email stemsee@chameleon-ai-agent-ltd.net. We will respond within one month.

If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), Wycliffe House, Water Lane, Wilmslow, Cheshire SK9 5AF — ico.org.uk — tel. 0303 123 1113. We would appreciate the chance to address your concerns first.

The website and Software are intended for business and adult personal use and are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18.
