UK chart of accounts, ready to go

Standard UK account codes out of the box, mapped to HMRC categories — assets, liabilities, equity, income, expenses. Add your own accounts whenever you need them.

🔍

It checks its own working

A forensic scan flags anything that doesn't add up — unbalanced entries, duplicate references, VAT mismatches — before it reaches a report.

📅

Knows the UK tax year

Year-end is 6 April to 5 April. Profit & Loss and VAT reports cover the right period automatically — no manual date wrangling.

🧾

MTD-shaped VAT returns

The VAT summary comes out box by box in the HMRC Making Tax Digital format: Box 1 output tax, Box 4 input tax, Box 6 net sales, Box 7 net purchases.

📈

The reports you actually file

Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet, VAT Return Summary — each broken down by account code. The Balance Sheet also confirms the books are whole: assets equal liabilities plus equity.

🧾

Invoices that pass

Every invoice carries all the fields HMRC Notice 700 requires — sequential number, both VAT numbers, dates of invoice and supply, itemised lines with per-line VAT, net and VAT subtotals. Print it, save a PDF, or push it to your phone. Set your company details once.

🔒

Your books stay yours

The whole ledger is a single file on your computer. It works with no internet. Run several companies side by side and switch between them with one flag.

🤖

Ask about your position

Every report also comes out as structured data the Chameleon AI can read — so you can ask, in plain English, how the quarter looks or where the VAT went.
