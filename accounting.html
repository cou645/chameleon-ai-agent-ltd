📚

UK chart of accounts, ready to go

Standard UK account codes out of the box, mapped to HMRC categories — assets, liabilities, equity, income, expenses. Add your own accounts whenever you need them.

🔍

It checks its own working

A forensic scan flags anything that doesn't add up — unbalanced entries, duplicate references, VAT mismatches — before it reaches a report.

📅

Knows the UK tax year

Year-end is 6 April to 5 April. Profit & Loss and VAT reports cover the right period automatically — no manual date wrangling.

📤

Files your VAT return to HMRC

Full Making Tax Digital for VAT: it reads your open obligations from HMRC, builds the nine-box return from the ledger, submits it, and retrieves the confirmation, your VAT liabilities and your payments — with HMRC's fraud-prevention headers. It won't let you file the same period twice.

📈

The reports you actually file

Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet, VAT Return Summary — each broken down by account code. The Balance Sheet also confirms the books are whole: assets equal liabilities plus equity.

🧾

Invoices that pass

Every invoice carries all the fields HMRC Notice 700 requires — sequential number, both VAT numbers, dates of invoice and supply, itemised lines with per-line VAT, net and VAT subtotals. Print it, save a PDF, or push it to your phone. Set your company details once.

🔒

Your books stay yours

The whole ledger lives on your own computer or phone, encrypted (SQLCipher v4 format, AES-256) with a key kept in your device's secure store. It works with no internet, keeps an encrypted backup each day, and never sends your books to Google or Apple backups. Export an ordinary copy whenever you choose.

📥

Import any UK bank statement

Add your Barclays, HSBC, Lloyds, Halifax, NatWest, Nationwide, Santander, Tide, Revolut, Starling, Monzo, Wise or PayPal account and import its CSV or OFX statement. Each line comes with a suggested category and VAT rate; nothing posts until you confirm it, and importing twice never doubles up.

⚖️

Standard, Cash and Flat Rate VAT

Choose your VAT scheme and the return follows it: invoice date for standard accounting, payment date for cash accounting, or your sector's percentage of VAT-inclusive turnover for the Flat Rate Scheme.

🖨️

Print, PDF and spreadsheets

Print your Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet, VAT summary and transactions, save them as PDF, or export CSV for Excel, LibreOffice Calc, Numbers or Google Sheets. On the desktop, keyboard shortcuts cover every section.

🏦

Open Banking bank feeds

Connect a bank account through a regulated Open Banking provider — GoCardless Bank Account Data or TrueLayer — and transactions arrive automatically. The connection runs on your machine; your bank data and credentials never pass through us.

⚖️

Reconciles against the feed

It matches each bank line to what's already in the books — by reference, by amount and date, or by payee — then shows what's left: transactions to post, and ledger entries the bank hasn't cleared. Re-importing never double-posts.

🪪

Checks the VAT and company numbers

Validates a UK VAT number's check digits before it ever reaches HMRC, and looks up a company's registered name, status and office on the Companies House register.

🤖

Ask about your position

Every report also comes out as structured data. Connect your own AI provider key — Anthropic, Google, or any OpenAI-compatible service — and you can ask, in plain English, how the quarter looks or where the VAT went. It only reads and drafts: it never posts an entry or files a return, and you check every figure. Optional, and off until you turn it on.

Check before you file

Deadlines, reliefs and a second pair of eyes on every quarter

⏰

Never miss a deadline

VAT returns, Companies House accounts and confirmation statement, Corporation Tax payment and CT600, and the quarterly updates of Making Tax Digital for Income Tax: worked out from your own company and VAT dates, shown on the dashboard, with reminders 30, 14, 7, 3 and 1 days before, then daily if one is late.

🧮

VAT back on unpaid invoices

When a customer hasn't paid an invoice six months after it was due, the app tells you, and claims back the VAT you already paid on it (bad debt relief, VAT Notice 700/18) in one step. If the customer pays later, the relief is paid back automatically.

✅

Review a quarter before filing

Built-in tests run on your device and send nothing: UK VAT charged on export sales, VAT reclaimed on entertaining, bad debt relief you could claim, the VAT registration threshold, whether cash accounting would help your cash flow, large capital purchases, and the anomaly scan. Each point comes with the HMRC guidance it is based on.

✨

AI review, your choice of detail

Optionally, ask your own AI provider to review the quarter too. You choose how much it sees: totals only, or every transaction. Its suggestions are marked as AI, not professional advice. For each point you choose: apply it (you see the VAT boxes before and after first), do it yourself, or dismiss it. Anything applied can be undone, and nothing is ever posted into a quarter you have already filed.

🕰️

A clock you can trust

The app checks the time against HMRC, Companies House and GOV.UK instead of relying on the device clock, and tells you if your clock is wrong. Deadlines, reviews and signed records all use the checked time.

🔐

Each company open only once

Each company's books can be open in only one copy of the app at a time, so two windows never work on the same figures. Run several companies side by side if you have them.

Works with your accountant

Optional. You stay in control of what is shared, and you always file yourself.

📨

Ask accountants for an offer

Open an accountant's card and send them a request for their service: up to seven accountants at once. Each request stays open for 48 hours, or 96 hours if sent on a Friday or Saturday, to allow for a bank holiday weekend. Accountants answer with an offer showing their fees in plain words; you accept one or refuse. Accepting one closes the other requests. An accountant can also invite you directly.

🤝

One accountant at a time

You link one accountant, and check their identity by reading a short code with them over the phone. Their terms and fees are signed by both of you before anything is shared. Change accountant, stop sending or end the link whenever you like.

✉️

Sealed, not uploaded

To send a quarter, you choose a summary or full detail and see exactly what will go before you press Send. The app seals it so only your accountant's app can open it, and you pass the file on by email, cloud drive or USB. We never see it. A file that has been tampered with, or comes from anyone else, is refused.

🧊

Frozen while under review

Sending a quarter freezes it and records a digital fingerprint of your books. You keep working as normal. Anything new dated in that quarter is marked, and you can send it to your accountant during their review. Their corrections apply to the frozen version, so they never cancel a later entry. What you file is the version you finished with, and you can withdraw a review at any time, so a deadline is never held up.

🔁

Your accountant's advice, applied your way

Your accountant's reply opens as a review. Apply each point, do it yourself or dismiss it, or replace your figures with their corrected version of the quarter: the app adds the entries that make your books match it exactly, without editing anything you entered. Record their bill in your books with one click.

💷

Fair fees when your accountant saves you money

If your accountant charges a share of the tax they save you, your own app measures that amount from the changes you actually made, and refuses any bill above what your agreed terms allow. Changes that add tax never count against you.

For accountants: practice mode

The same app, used by an accountant with many clients

🗂️

Clients and new requests

Share your card with your fees, money-laundering supervisor and insurance. Requests from new clients arrive with the time left to answer; make an offer or decline. Your client list shows who is active, paused or ended, and when each client's records may be deleted.

🔎

Review with built-in tests and AI

A client's quarter opens on your screen with the same built-in tests and an optional AI draft. Edit, add or delete points, and confirm each one yourself: nothing you haven't confirmed can be sent. Prepare a corrected version of the whole quarter and see the VAT boxes change as you edit.

🧾

Bills from your terms

Fees per review, by the hour or as a share of savings, with time records for each client. Your bill is suggested from the terms the client accepted; change your terms and clients accept the new version before you bill under it.

🗄️

Each client's data kept separately

Every client's data is in its own encrypted file with its own key, separate from your own books. Back up to a USB drive: the copies stay encrypted, with no keys and no client names on the drive. A passphrase-locked recovery kit restores everything on a new computer. When records are no longer needed, deleting a client destroys their key, so every copy becomes unreadable.

The quarterly VAT cycle, end to end

The Open Banking feed brings in the quarter's bank transactions; CSV and OFX statement imports work too, and receipts arrive from the phone. Reconciliation matches them against invoices and expenses already entered.

🧮

How it is built and tested

Tax software has to be right, so most of the work goes into proving it

Chameleon Accounting MTD is written in Dart and Flutter, so one code base can run on Linux, Windows, macOS, Android and iOS. Marcos designs it and decides what it must do; the code is written by AI under that direction. Nothing is taken on trust: more than 500 automated tests run against each change. Some of the checks plant a deliberate error to prove that the checks really can fail. An accountant has not yet reviewed the tax rules, and that will happen before release.

🏛️

1. Every day against HMRC's sandbox

HMRC runs a practice copy of its VAT service for software developers, called the sandbox. Since 29 September 2026 the app has connected to it automatically, up to three times a day, to check its VAT calls and the fraud prevention details HMRC requires with every call. By 4 November 2026 that was 124 runs, of which 108 passed, with at least one pass on every one of the 37 days. We have applied to HMRC for permission to use the live service and are waiting for its decision. The sandbox tests our software, not your real tax records.

⏩

2. A tax year in a day

A sandbox can't show what happens over a whole year, so we built our own simulator. A fake clock runs a made-up company through its year, with stand-ins for HMRC, Companies House and an accountant, while the app's real code does the work. A separate answer key, sharing no code with the app, works out what every figure should be. Run flat out, a year takes seconds. You can also watch the real app run at 365 times normal speed: a year in a day, under a red banner saying it isn't real.

🏢

What the simulator covers

Different kinds of business: small profits, marginal relief, a loss, cash accounting, the Flat Rate Scheme, a year end that doesn't match the VAT quarters, a director who doesn't repay a loan, a company crossing the VAT limit, and a sole trader. It also plays out what goes wrong: planted bookkeeping mistakes, HMRC being down, an accountant replying late or leaving during a review, and a company's year end changing.

🐞

What it has found

Real bugs, all now fixed. A balance sheet stopped balancing after 31 December. A cheque crossing two quarters upset the bank check. Corporation Tax ignored VAT kept under the Flat Rate Scheme. The warning about the VAT limit never appeared for the businesses that needed it. A tax return reminder vanished three months before the return was due. Countdowns were a day short across the spring clock change. Finding these was the reason for building it.

📐

Checked against the official rules

Where official rules are published, we test against them directly. Draft Company Tax Returns are checked offline against HMRC's own published structure and business rules. Draft accounts and tax computations are checked against the official tag lists using Arelle, a free, independent checking tool. A simulator can only prove what we believe about a service, so this is no substitute for HMRC's own test services, which have not yet seen these documents.

📑

3. Coming next, potentially: statutory accounts

We have working drafts, not yet in any release, of the annual accounts a micro-entity (FRS 105) or small company (FRS 102 Section 1A) must prepare, a tax computation in HMRC's prescribed layout, and a Company Tax Return (CT600). They could let a small company do its year end in one place. This app cannot file any of them today, and we make no promise that it will. That depends on an accountant's review, on HMRC recognising the software for Company Tax Returns, on passing HMRC's test services, and on Companies House's own requirements.

Linux users: standard kernel recommended

Chameleon Accounting MTD is built and tested on the standard kernel every major Linux distribution installs. On a full real-time kernel (PREEMPT_RT, often named -rt) we have seen the system freeze and crash in the kernel's real-time locking code while running Dart and Flutter software; the standard kernel of the same version was stable. If you use a real-time kernel and see freezes, switch to your distribution's standard kernel (for low latency, its preempt=full boot option is a safer choice). uname -v shows PREEMPT_RT on a real-time kernel. This has nothing to do with the app's encryption.
