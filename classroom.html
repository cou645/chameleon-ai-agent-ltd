Whiteboard over any document

Draw and annotate on top of a live PDF, an image, a ClassIn .edb board or a Xournal++ .xopp file. A narration overlay keeps your voice and your pen together, so students follow both.

🎭

A slide player in the room

The lower panel is a built-in Kameleon Presenter, play-only — run your .kpt decks inside the lesson without leaving it.

🖥️

Video wall with staging

Every student is a tile. Bring any one to the stage to focus the class on them — for a reading, a correction, a role-play.

🤖

An AI in the lesson

An AI participant sits in the chat. A Send to AI button — on the teacher app and on every student's phone — pulls it in for a definition, an example sentence, a quick check.

🗣️

Pronunciation drilling

A built-in pronunciation widget for practising sounds and words.

🔒

Private connections, no video bill

Video runs directly between participants over your own private network — no third-party video service and no per-minute charge. It falls back to local-network video when everyone is in one building.

📇

Enrolment and payment

An enrolment record tracks who's in which class. Take payment with Stripe, Alipay or PayPal.

📱

Students join from their phone

Students connect through the Chameleon Classroom companion app on Android — video, chat, and the same Send to AI button.
