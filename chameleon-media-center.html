Chameleon Media Center

A full desktop media studio built on mpv — playback, playlists, live streaming, sound effects, a visualizer, a video editor, an equalizer, and phone mirroring, in one app.

🎞️ mpv-powered playback 📻 Live streaming 🎚️ Equalizer & effects 📱 Phone mirroring (ADB) 📲 Remote-controllable

What's inside

Nine tabs, one media engine

🎞️

Video panel

Playback powered by mpv — wide format support, smooth seeking, reliable everywhere else it's used in the Chameleon AI stack.

📃

Playlist

Drag-and-drop reordering, queue management, quick access to what's next.

📡

Live Stream

Pull in a live stream alongside a built-in Sound Effects board for on-the-fly audio cues.

🌈

Visualizer

Real-time audio-reactive visuals for anything currently playing.

✂️

Video editor

Trim, cut and assemble clips without leaving the app.

🎚️

Equalizer & audio effects

Shape the sound to the room, or the mood.

✅

ToDo tab

A lightweight task list, right alongside your media — useful for production/session notes while you work.

📱

ADB Phone tab

Mirror and control an Android device over ADB — screen and audio — directly inside the media center.

📲

Remote control

Every play/pause/seek/volume action is exposed over mpv's own IPC socket, which TrXi-Ctrl — our free phone app — talks to directly for real transport control, not just a session list.

Built on open foundations

PySide6 (Qt) desktop app, mpv playback engine

🖼️

PySide6 / Qt

A native, responsive desktop UI — part of the same Python/Qt stack as the rest of the Chameleon AI desktop apps.

🎬

mpv engine

Embedded via mpv's own window-embedding and JSON IPC socket — the same reliable, widely-used playback engine behind many Linux media tools.
